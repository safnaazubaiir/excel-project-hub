  **SUPER MARKET SALES ANALYSIS**

This project analyzes supermarket sales data to understand overall sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, branch performance, payment methods, monthly sales trends, and customer ratings. The analysis is performed using Microsoft Excel to identify useful business insights and support data-driven decisions

**BUSINESS QUESTIONS**
1)	What is the total sales amount for each branch?
2)	Which product line contributes the most to total sales?
3)	Which month has the highest sale?
4)  Which product line has the highest quantity sold?
5)	Which product line receives the highest average rating?
6)	What is the sales distribution between male &female customers?

**CHARTS**

• Sales by Branch: Compares total sales across branches and helps identify branch-level performance
• Sales by Product Line: Shows the sales contribution of each product line
• Monthly Sales Trend: Shows how sales change from month to month and identifies high- and low-sales periods
• Quantity Sold by Product Line: Compares units sold across product lines
• Average Rating by Product Line: Compares customer ratings across product lines
• Sales by Gender: Shows the sales distribution between male and female customers

 **SLICERS & TIMELINE**
 
• Branch – filters the dashboard by branch
• Product Line – filters the dashboard by product category
• Customer Type – filters the analysis by customer type
• Gender – filters sales based on customer gender
• Timeline - Provides up-to-date sales informations for timely decision-making 

**KPI CARDS**

• Total Sales: Displays the overall sales amount generated from supermarket transactions
• Total Quantity: Shows the total number of units sold
• Average Rating: Displays the average customer rating 

**KEY FINDINGS**

• Brooklyn has the highest total sales with 342,844.41
• Home & Lifestyle contributes the most to total sales with 207,123.59
• April has the highest sales with 140,766.38
• Food & Beverages has the highest quantity sold with 7,309 units
• Food & Beverages has the highest average rating with 7.08
• Female customers have higher sales than male customers

 **RECOMMENDATIONS**
 
• Maintain the successful sales strategies in Brooklyn and identify practices that can be applied to the other branches
• Maintain sufficient stock of Home & Lifestyle products and continue promoting high-performing products
• Plan promotions and stock levels according to high-sales months and use special offers during lower-sales months
• Ensure sufficient stock of Food & Beverages products and promote popular items to maintain high demand
• Continue maintaining the quality of Food & Beverages products and use customer feedback to improve other product lines
• Continue targeting female customers while also creating suitable promotions to increase sales among male customers
